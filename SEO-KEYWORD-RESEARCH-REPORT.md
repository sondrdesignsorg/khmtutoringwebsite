## Executive summary

KHM has a solid technical and service-page base: dedicated pages exist for math, SAT, SSAT, AP, college essays, online tutoring, private-school entrance prep, and group SAT prep. The prior production crawl (November 10–11) found all 15 sitemap URLs returning 200 with titles, descriptions, canonicals, one H1, and indexability; sitemap, robots.txt, and llms.txt also responded successfully. The homepage and service pages are already aligned with high-intent Honolulu/Hawaii searches.

The greatest opportunity is to turn this coverage into measurable organic acquisition. Search Console, Google Business Profile, GA4, and a paid keyword-data source were not connected in this review, so **current query impressions, clicks, rankings, conversion rates, monthly search volumes, keyword difficulty, and backlinks are unknown**. Keyword priorities below are strategic estimates based on local intent, live search-result sampling, service fit, and conversion potential—not measured volume or ranking data.

Three immediate concerns surfaced in the live search snapshot: (1) the homepage result currently exposes an outdated Gmail address despite the current business email being `info@khmtutoring.com`; (2) live contact-page content includes inconsistent service-area/hours claims compared with the verified business configuration and recent location copy; and (3) the homepage uses several prominent numerical and rating claims whose source/refresh date the prior audit could not validate. Fix public fact consistency before expanding content.

## 1. Method and confidence

I reviewed the repository’s metadata and service content, the existing production technical audit, live indexed pages and search snippets, and a sample of local tutoring competitors and adjacent results. Search-result sampling is directional: results vary by location, device, personalization, and time, and a result appearing for a query does not establish its rank or traffic.

**Keyword priority scale:**

- **P1:** strong commercial fit and/or clear local intent; prioritize page optimization and internal links.
- **P2:** relevant supporting intent; address on a service page, FAQ, or focused guide.
- **P3:** useful long-tail or discovery topic; publish only when KHM can add specific, first-hand value.

**Demand notation:** “Local intent; validate volume” means the phrase is plausible and commercially aligned, but no reliable Hawaii monthly search estimate was available. Use Search Console for observed demand and Google Ads Keyword Planner configured to Honolulu/Oʻahu and Hawaii for directional historical volume. Google describes historical metrics as estimates for past Google Search performance; Keyword Planner forecasts are not organic traffic forecasts ([Google Ads API historical metrics](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/keyword-planning/generate-historical-metrics), [Keyword Planner guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243/use-keyword-planner)).

## 5. On-page and content recommendations

### P1: Resolve public business-data drift

The current business configuration records `info@khmtutoring.com`, appointment-based hours, and an in-person corridor from Pearl City to Hawaiʻi Kai plus statewide online delivery. Yet the live homepage search snippet showed `khmtutoring1@gmail.com`, while the live contact page reported different areas and hours, including statewide in-home and 8 AM–10 PM availability. The recent audit also noted that older customer-facing email text had been corrected, but this current search result still suggests a remaining public surface or stale index. Reconcile footer, contact FAQ, homepage, metadata, structured data, `llms.txt`, and directory profiles to one approved source. Verify current pages directly and request recrawl after correction.

### P1: Strengthen the location entity and Google Business Profile

Confirm business eligibility and exact operating model before editing profile details. Google's guidance says a business showing its address should maintain permanent fixed signage; service-area businesses should represent their service area accurately ([Google Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177)). KHM's partner-cafe/private-after-2-PM setup is distinctive, but all location copy and any profile must accurately explain access and customer-facing availability. Audit primary category, business name, phone, URL, address/service areas, appointment requirements, hours, photos, and review responses. Keep directory NAP consistent.

### P1: Refine page titles, descriptions, and calls to action

Service pages have good intent-specific titles and detailed copy. Check every live title/description against the approved positioning and page content after data cleanup. Include the target service and geography in clear natural language; make the snippet benefit-specific (e.g., individual vs group SAT, course levels, diagnostic) rather than repeating generic “expert tutoring” text. Google may rewrite title links and snippets from page content, so the visible H1 and opening copy should reinforce the same subject and location ([Google SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)).

### P1: Ensure each high-intent page has visible proof and next step

On SAT, math, SSAT, AP, group SAT, and essay pages, show: who the service fits, what is covered, tutor match/credentials relevant to that service, where/how sessions happen, realistic process/timing, FAQs, and a direct consultation/diagnostic CTA. Use page-specific testimonial evidence only when permission, attribution, and current status are documented. Maintain a claim register for “300+,” “+15%,” “5.0,” “10+,” and elite-school/credential statements; attach source, calculation, date checked, and owner.

### P2: Publish a small, high-value local resource hub

Build four to six genuinely useful resources instead of a generic blog volume push:

1. **Hawaii private-school entrance prep calendar** — SSAT/ISEE basics, planning milestones, and school-specific links; check annually against official admissions pages.
2. **Punahou/Iolani SSAT prep guide** — exam levels, sections, how to choose a prep timeline, and what tutoring can/cannot do. Avoid promises about admission.
3. **Digital SAT prep plan for Honolulu students** — diagnostic-to-study-plan example, adaptive test format, practice cadence, and the distinction between class and one-to-one prep.
4. **AP exam tutoring timeline** — course support through exam review, with separate pages or sections only for subjects KHM can staff.
5. **Online tutoring across Hawaiʻi** — connection/session logistics, time zones, subject options, and how families receive progress updates.
6. **Choosing a math tutor in Honolulu** — diagnostic, tutor match, learning goals, and questions families should ask.

Each resource should link contextually to its service page, show author/reviewer and updated date, and include original local expertise. Do not create thin pages for every school, neighborhood, grade, or keyword variant.

### P2: Build internal links around user pathways

- Homepage → primary service pages, with group SAT separated from one-to-one SAT.
- Math ↔ AP Calculus and SAT Math; SAT ↔ group SAT and online tutoring.
- SSAT ↔ private-school admissions prep and online tutoring.
- College essays ↔ private-school application essays only where intent distinction is clear.
- Educator profiles link to the subjects each tutor actually teaches.
- Relevant resource guides link to the service page and one next-step CTA.

Use descriptive link text such as “Honolulu math tutoring” or “small-group SAT prep,” avoiding repeated generic “learn more.”

### Technical / structured data

The prior audit found valid business, website, service, tutor/person, and breadcrumb JSON-LD in the checked pages. Keep markup synchronized with visible, verified facts. Do not restore self-serving review/aggregate-rating markup: Google says reviews about an entity controlled by that entity are ineligible for review snippets on LocalBusiness/Organization pages ([Google review snippet policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet)). Recheck sitemap freshness when content changes; the source currently assigns static last-modified dates to service routes. Avoid claiming performance or rich results from the presence of schema alone.

Re-run a crawl after copy fixes for status, indexability, canonical, title, description, H1, internal links, broken links, image alt text, and structured data. Measure mobile LCP, INP, CLS and image payload on homepage, SAT, and math pages when PageSpeed access is available.

## 6. Local SEO and reputation plan

1. Verify Google Business Profile owner access and determine whether the physical location qualifies for a visible address under current guidelines. Make the profile match the actual private/appointment access model.
2. Reconcile name, phone, URL, address, hours, appointment instructions, and service area across Yelp, LinkedIn, local directories, and school/community references.
3. Add fresh original photos of tutors, teaching, and the location when permitted; caption/accessibility text should describe the image accurately, not stuff keywords.
4. Request honest feedback from actual families through the normal review process; never gate reviews or offer incentives. Respond helpfully without revealing student information.
5. Track calls, contact submissions, and diagnostic completions by landing page. Organic visibility without lead-quality measurement cannot establish business impact.

### KPI definitions

- **Visibility:** nonbrand impressions and average position by service cluster and destination page.
- **Traffic quality:** organic clicks, CTR, engaged sessions, and landing-page engagement by device.
- **Leads:** qualified form submissions, calls, booked consultations, and diagnostic completions attributed to organic entry pages.
- **Local:** map/search views and calls, website clicks, direction requests where applicable, review count/recency, and profile accuracy.
- **Technical:** indexed intended URLs, crawl errors, mobile Core Web Vitals, and sitemap processing.

Do not set numerical growth targets until a baseline is available. Compare year over year where possible because SAT, AP, and admissions needs have seasonal demand.
